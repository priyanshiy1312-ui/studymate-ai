# 3-minute demo script

**0:00 The problem (15s).** "Lectures, textbooks and slides live in different places, and chatbots answer without showing where it came from. StudyMate fixes that."

**0:15 Ingest (20s).** Show Your materials with the sample course loaded: lecture transcript, textbook chapter, slide deck. Optionally paste a YouTube link. Point out the passage counts.

**0:35 Cited answer (45s).** Tutor tab. Ask: "Why does a large learning rate hurt training?" Click a citation chip: the viewer opens the slide / lecture timestamp. "Every claim links to its source."

**1:20 Honesty (15s).** Ask something not in the materials ("Who won the 2018 World Cup?"). It says it can't find it and refuses to guess. Mention the confidence score.

**1:35 Socratic tutoring (25s).** Ask about a topic you haven't practised: it gives a hint and a guiding question. Click Show full answer.

**2:00 Adaptive practice (35s).** Practice tab: answer a question. Show the graded feedback with a source citation and the mastery change. Answer a second one to show difficulty and topic adapting.

**2:35 Progress (20s).** Progress tab: heatmap, weak topics, next-session plan with source links.

**2:55 Close (5s).** "Measured citation accuracy is in eval/results.json. StudyMate: study from your own sources, with proof."
